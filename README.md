# AbyssalScope
### Ocean Threat Monitoring System

AbyssalScope is an AI-powered ocean threat monitoring platform that gives the world's most endangered ocean regions a voice. Built for FullyHacks 2026 under the **Social Impact & Sustainability** track.

---

## What It Does

Select any ocean region on the interactive map and AbyssalScope will:

1. **Deploy a submarine** to navigate to your selected region
2. **Generate a first-person distress transmission** from that body of water — powered by Claude AI, grounded in real scientific data
3. **Open a live dialogue** where you can talk back to the ocean, ask questions, and find out what you can do
4. **Let you submit field reports** of coastal issues you've personally witnessed

Every statistic the ocean cites is sourced from peer-reviewed studies and government agencies. The AI gives the data a voice — it does not invent the facts.

---

##  Features

-  **Interactive threat map** — 20 ocean regions across every major basin, color-coded by threat type
-  **AI ocean monologue** — Claude generates a urgent, fact-based distress transmission for each region
-  **Two-way dialogue** — talk back to the ocean and get responses grounded in real data
-  **Threat legend** — global ocean health score + breakdown by threat type
-  **Community field reports** — submit and view real coastal observations from around the world
-  **Submarine animation** — animated submarine navigates to your selected region
-  **Light/dark mode** — cinematic HUD dark mode or bright colorful map
-  **Live HUD stats** — real-time ocean health score, field report count, regions tracked

##  Data Sources

All ocean health data is sourced from verified scientific institutions:

- **NOAA Coral Reef Watch** — coral bleaching and sea surface temperatures
- **Australian Institute of Marine Science (AIMS)** — Great Barrier Reef monitoring
- **National Snow and Ice Data Center (NSIDC)** — Arctic sea ice decline
- **NASA Earthdata** — ocean temperature anomalies
- **WWF Living Planet Report** — biodiversity and overfishing data
- **Mekong River Commission** — Mekong fisheries data
- **IUCN Red List** — endangered marine species
- **Conservation International** — ecosystem health assessments
- **Oceana UK** — North Sea pollution data
- **UN Environment Programme** — global ocean health
